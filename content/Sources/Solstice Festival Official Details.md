## Summary

The official Solstice pages make the festival a concrete route anchor: Solstice 2026 takes place 2026-06-18 through 2026-06-20 in [[Entities/Ruka]], Kuusamo, at Rukatunturi / Ruka Ski Resort. The festival site is a fell-top environment with rocky, uneven terrain and level changes. Official guidance describes access by car, charter bus, train plus onward bus, or flight to Kuusamo or Oulu.

For the roadtrip, the operational consequences are significant. The rental-car loop must now reach Ruka before the festival, preserve enough recovery time after the festival, and return to Helsinki by 2026-06-25. The festival itself is not a camping site; accommodation, parking, lift access, walking distances, and late-night return from the festival site need to be planned around Ruka's layout.

## Why It Matters

Before this ingest, [[Entities/Solstice Festival]] was fixed but geographically underspecified. The official pages now locate the anchor in northern Finland and add constraints that affect routing, parking, packing, food/water, local movement, and post-festival recovery. Ruka is far enough from Helsinki that the pre- and post-festival route should be treated as a real route design problem rather than a short transfer.

## Key Operational Facts

- Dates: Solstice 2026 is listed for 2026-06-18 through 2026-06-20.
- Location: Rukatunturi / Ruka Ski Resort in [[Entities/Ruka]], Kuusamo, Finland.
- Ticketing page venue detail: Rukatunturintie 9, 93825 Ruka, Finland.
- Festival site: fell-top / mountain environment, roughly 490-500 meters high, rocky, uneven, and not fully wheelchair accessible.
- Hours: festival hours are listed as 16:00-02:00, with official After Hours 01:00-06:00 at ski lodge Piste in Ruka Village.
- Movement: local movement is mainly by foot and ski lift. Three-day festival tickets include a ski-lift ticket; one-day tickets do not.
- Late-night movement: the ski lifts close at 20:00, so after the festival area closes at 02:00, attendees walk down on a marked road; the FAQ describes this as roughly a 15-minute walk.
- Driving and parking: official guidance says driving is possible and that there are parking spaces around Ruka Village and by some accommodations, with additional parking information linked to Ruka.
- Public/charter transport: Solstice lists charter buses from Helsinki and Oulu, Matkahuolto regional buses, train to Oulu with onward bus, and flights to Kuusamo or Oulu.
- Accommodation: Solstice-linked accommodation options are described as within walking distance of the festival area, with some options close to the ski lift. Towels, bed linen, and end-of-stay cleaning are included for the listed accommodation options.
- Camping: the FAQ says there is no camping on the festival site or organized by the festival. It points to Valtavaara as the nearest tent-camping possibility.
- Food and water: outside drinks and meals are not allowed at the festival site; small snacks and an empty non-deposit water bottle are allowed. Drinkable water is available inside the festival area.
- Local services: the FAQ names Ruka Village services including an ATM, pharmacy service point, grocery store, and additional shops at Rukan Portti.
- Gear: official guidance recommends warm layers, wind- and waterproof jacket and pants, suitable shoes for challenging terrain, mosquito protection, sunscreen, and sun protection.
- Prohibited items include pets/animals, umbrellas, portable chairs, aerosol sprays, Thermacell mosquito repellents, deposit bottles, and professional camera/tripod/selfie-stick style gear.

## Planning Implications

- The route comparison should now treat [[Entities/Ruka]] as the mid-trip geographic anchor.
- The pre-festival route has to arrive before 2026-06-17 if the house booking starts then, but the public festival itself begins on 2026-06-18.
- Post-festival planning depends heavily on the house checkout date: 2026-06-21 creates a tighter recovery/transfer problem than 2026-06-22.
- Camping cannot be assumed around the festival site. If a tent option near Ruka matters, verify Valtavaara / Nationalparks rules separately.
- The festival requires practical packing: waterproof layers, real shoes, mosquito protection, sun protection, and an empty water bottle.
- A car can be useful around Ruka, but local festival movement is still mostly foot/ski-lift/taxi/e-bike rather than driving to the fell-top site.
- Tickets, accommodation terms, After Hours tickets, and any accommodation cancellation rules should be checked against the actual bookings.
