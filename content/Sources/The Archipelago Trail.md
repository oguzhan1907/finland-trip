# The Archipelago Trail

## Metadata

- File: `raw/The Archipelago Trail.md`
- Type: official / promotional route page clipping
- Date: 2024-05-06 in clipped frontmatter; page text references the 2026 season
- Author / Organization: Archipelago Trail / Pargas tourist information

## Summary

This source describes the Archipelago Trail as a 250 km coastal route through the Turku archipelago, linked by ferry crossings and usable by car, bike, boat, or on foot. For the 2026 trip, the key claim is that the full trail can be explored from 2026-05-08 through 2026-09-13, which overlaps the roadtrip window from 2026-06-04 through 2026-06-26.

The route is especially relevant because it creates a distinctive Finland segment that is more than a drive between sights: the ferries, islands, coastal villages, and detours are part of the route itself. It also creates operational dependencies that need current ferry timetable verification before being treated as booked or guaranteed.

## Why It Matters

- It is the first route source in the vault that is both scenic and ferry-linked.
- It fits the June 2026 trip window according to the source text.
- It suggests a 3-5 day route module that could anchor the southern/coastal part of the trip.
- It creates clear follow-up research needs for ferry timetables, car capacity, lodging, and route direction.

## Key Points

- The full trail is described as 250 km through Pargas, Nagu, Korpo, Houtskär, Iniö, Kustavi, Taivassalo, Vehmaa, Mynämäki, and Naantali.
- The source recommends at least 3-5 days for the route.
- The trail is described as a mix of nature, culture, history, seaside towns, wilderness trails, cobblestone streets, and coastal life.
- Islands and mainland sections are linked by ferry crossings, most of them described as free.
- The source calls out the smaller 120 km Small Archipelago Trail as an alternative if the full 250 km route is too long.
- The source frames detours into the outer archipelago as a major part of the route, including Nötö, Aspö, Jurmo, and Utö.

## Important Claims Or Figures

- Full 2026 trail season: 2026-05-08 through 2026-09-13.
- Full route length: 250 km.
- Small Archipelago Trail length: 120 km.
- Suggested journey length: at least 3-5 days.
- Korpo-Houtskär leg includes three ferries according to the source: one around 30 minutes and two short crossings.
- The Galtby-Kittuis ferry is described as taking around 30 minutes and departing every 1-2 hours, but this must be checked against current official timetables before planning a drive day around it.

## Route Legs Mentioned

- Åbo / Turku to Pargas: 25 km.
- Pargas to Nagu: 35 km.
- Nagu to Korpo: 23 km.
- Korpo to Houtskär: 24 km.
- Houtskär to Iniö: 30 km.
- Iniö to Kustavi / Taivassalo: 30 km.
- Taivassalo via Mynämäki or Velkua toward Merimasku.
- Merimasku to Naantali: 11 km.
- Naantali to Turku: 17 km.
