## Summary

This ingest uses the 11th edition as a broad route cross-check for the Helsinki-starting Finland roadtrip, not as a full guidebook extraction. The useful material is the way the guide organizes Finland into route-sized modules: southern cities and coasts, [[Entities/Saimaa]] and Lakeland, [[Entities/Koli National Park]] / [[Entities/Kuhmo]] / [[Entities/Kainuu]], [[Entities/Oulu]] and the west coast, [[Entities/Oulanka National Park]] / [[Entities/Ruka]] / [[Entities/Hossa National Park]], and longer [[Entities/Lapland]] extensions.

The guidebook strengthens the current eastern-first hypothesis but does not make any route operational by itself. Schedules, prices, provider availability, road timings, event calendars, park rules, ferry details, and lodging availability still need current official or provider checks before booking.

- It helps separate route modules that could fit the 2026-06-06 to 2026-06-25 car window from long-distance northern ideas that would consume too much slack.

## Key Route Material

### Route-Framework Itineraries

- **Essential Suomi**: a 7-day / 957 km south-and-central loop linking [[Entities/Helsinki]], Porvoo, [[Entities/Saimaa]], [[Entities/Tampere]], Rauma, and [[Entities/Turku]]. For this vault, it supports Helsinki/Saimaa/Tampere/Turku as proven guidebook route modules, but the current Ruka anchor means it cannot simply be copied.
- **Cities & Coasts**: a 7-day / 661 km southern city-and-coast route through [[Entities/Helsinki]], Hanko, [[Entities/Turku]], Rauma, Hameenlinna, and Porvoo. It supports a culture/coast fallback or early southern add-on, especially if weather or pace argues against a more remote start.
- **Call of the Wild**: a 12-day / 1420 km north-and-wilderness route beginning at [[Entities/Koli National Park]] and moving through [[Entities/Kuhmo]], [[Entities/Oulanka National Park]], Rovaniemi, Inari, and Kilpisjarvi. It is too large to graft onto the current route, but it validates Koli/Kuhmo/Oulanka as a coherent wild/northern corridor.

### Saimaa And Lakeland

The guidebook treats [[Entities/Saimaa]] / Lakeland as a major roadtrip region rather than a single destination. Its route logic includes [[Entities/Savonlinna]], Punkaharju, Mikkeli, Puumala / Sahanlahti, Road 62 / Lietvesi, [[Entities/Linnansaari National Park]], [[Entities/Kolovesi National Park]], and Lappeenranta as lake-and-town modules.

For this vault, the Saimaa material strengthens the shared trunk for all three eastern route skeletons. It also gives the best guidebook evidence for pairing lakes, cottages, boat trips, castles, ridges, and scenic roads before the route turns north toward Kainuu or Ruka.

### Koli, Kuhmo, And Kainuu

The guidebook is strong support for Option A: [[Entities/Saimaa]] -> [[Entities/Koli National Park]] -> [[Entities/Kuhmo]] / [[Entities/Kainuu]] -> [[Entities/Ruka]]. It frames Koli as a major scenic/national-landscape stop and Kuhmo as a wilderness, wildlife, Kalevala, and Winter War history node in southeastern Kainuu.

This does not settle the option. It raises Option A's reward side, but the proof burden remains drive-time and staging: Koli/Kuhmo must add enough distinct value before the Ruka deadline.

### Oulanka, Ruka, And Hossa

The guidebook frames [[Entities/Oulanka National Park]] as a major Ruka-adjacent outdoor area with canyons, rapids, long-distance hiking, day hikes, and river activity. It also notes that lodging and services in or near the park are limited compared with [[Entities/Ruka]].

For [[Entities/Hossa National Park]], it supports the current source-pack signal: less visited than Oulanka, lake/river/forest character, rock paintings, hiking, cycling, canoeing, a visitor centre, and nearby campground/cabin infrastructure. Current rules, rental details, and availability still need official/provider verification.

### West Coast And Oulu

The west-coast chapter supports the western / Oulu corridor as a legitimate route family rather than just a map fallback. It links coastal towns, islands, Kvarken context, [[Entities/Oulu]], Hailuoto, and a possible continuation to Oulanka.

For the current trip, this is a comparison path: valuable if coast/Oulu culture becomes important, but less directly aligned with sheltered lake/cabin/hut interests than the eastern route.

### South Coast, Turku, And Southern Cities

The Turku / South Coast material supports southern culture and coast as a flexible add-on: [[Entities/Turku]], Hanko, Porvoo, Rauma, Hameenlinna, and Helsinki-adjacent city/culture stops. These are useful for first or last days, bad weather, or if the route needs to protect slack.

### North Lapland

The Lapland material supports Rovaniemi, Inari, Kilpisjarvi, and farther-north routes as high-distinctiveness travel ideas. In this vault, the main effect is pruning discipline: north beyond Ruka is probably a separate priority, not an automatic add-on to the Solstice trip.
