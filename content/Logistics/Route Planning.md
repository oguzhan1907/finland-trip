## Working Route Principles

- Avoid making every day a transfer day.
- Protect one or more flexible days for weather, fatigue, or route changes.
- Keep ferry arrivals and long drives from stacking on the same day unless necessary.
- Use confirmed bookings as route anchors only after cancellation rules are known.
- Treat source article route lengths as rough planning modules until mapped against the actual Helsinki-to-Ruka-to-Helsinki loop.
- Treat guidebook route lengths and provider leads as planning modules until checked against current maps, official pages, and June 2026 availability.

## First Route Families To Compare

- [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]]: compact, distinctive, ferry-linked coastal module; needs ferry and lodging verification.
- Savo-Karelia / [[Entities/Saimaa]]: major inland route family with strong nature/culture clustering; likely needs pruning if combined with coast.
- [[Entities/Koli National Park]] / [[Entities/Kuhmo]] / [[Entities/Kainuu]]: scenic eastern branch with strong guidebook value and high drive-stage proof burden.
- [[Entities/Hossa National Park]] / [[Entities/Oulanka National Park]]: activity-oriented branch where Hossa now needs provider/drive-stage proof and Oulanka needs a checkout/fatigue/crowd fit check.
- West Coast / [[Entities/Oulu]]: coastal route family that may pair with Hailuoto/Oulanka logic.
- South Coast design and ironworks: short cultural module near Helsinki/Turku.
- [[Entities/Lapland]]: high-distinctiveness route family with the highest distance/pace risk; currently likely unrealistic unless prioritized.
- Ruka / Kuusamo anchor: no longer optional because of [[Entities/Solstice Festival]]. Compare approaches via Saimaa/eastern Finland, Oulu/West Coast, or a more direct staged route with enough rest before the festival.
