# Route Relevant National Parks

## Question

Which Finnish national parks are relevant enough to maintain as pages in this vault right now?

## Short Answer

Do not add every Finnish national park. Maintain the parks that are already connected to the current route families: Saimaa/Lakeland, eastern Koli/Kainuu, Hossa/Oulanka/Ruka, the Turku archipelago, the West Coast/Oulu comparison, and the North Lapland comparison.

## Candidate Later Pages

These parks may deserve pages later, but they are not as strongly supported by the current route decision:

- Nuuksio or Sipoonkorpi if Helsinki nature days become important.
- Teijo or Ekenas Archipelago if the South Coast design route becomes a real branch.
- Repovesi or Paijanne if the central/direct return needs a nature stop.
- Syote or Riisitunturi if the Oulu / Kuusamo / Ruka area becomes a larger outdoor route rather than just a festival anchor.
- Lemmenjoki, Pallas-Yllastunturi, or Pyha-Luosto only if the trip pivots toward North Lapland.

## Planning Implication

The next useful park ingest is not a broad national-park encyclopedia. It is destination-specific official source work for the parks most likely to affect bookings and route shape:

- Hossa provider and route-stage checks first if Eastern B remains strongest; the official/provider source pass is already captured in [[Sources/Hossa Oulanka Official Source Pack]].
- [[Entities/Kolovesi National Park]] and [[Entities/Linnansaari National Park]] if the Saimaa trunk needs a concrete lake/paddling decision.
- [[Entities/Archipelago National Park]] only if the Small Archipelago Trail becomes a real early-trip module.
