## Triggers To Book

- A ferry creates a fixed arrival or departure point.
- The [[Entities/Solstice Festival]] checkout date is confirmed.
- The exact [[Entities/Ruka]] house location, parking, and check-in path are confirmed.
- A region has limited lodging.
- A must-see stop depends on staying nearby.
- Prices or availability start tightening.
- A ferry-linked route would become awkward if the nearest overnight options are full.
- A remote route family remains in scope after route-family comparison.
- [[Entities/Hossa National Park]] remains the lead Eastern B anchor after drive-stage mapping.
- A hut-based hike becomes more than an opportunistic idea.
- A paid shared rental hut or rental hut is needed to make a remote stop reliable.
- A kayaking, packrafting, or remote island stay requires a provider, boat transfer, gear pickup, or weather-specific booking.
