# Finland Trip Overview

## Confirmed Frame

- Finland trip window: Thursday 2026-06-04 through Friday 2026-06-26.
- Arrival mode: by plane to Helsinki.
- Roadtrip frame: starts and ends in Helsinki.
- Car window: rental car planned for 2026-06-06 through 2026-06-25, with pickup at Helsinki Airport.
- Helsinki frame: carless Helsinki days at the beginning and end of the trip.
- Fixed anchor: [[Entities/Solstice Festival]] in [[Entities/Ruka]], Kuusamo. The public festival is 2026-06-18 through 2026-06-20, with a house booked from 2026-06-17 through either 2026-06-21 or 2026-06-22.
- Trip party: two people on the way to the festival and two people on the way back from the festival.
- Planning status: research-first; route and most bookings are intentionally still open.

## Planning Priorities

1. Build a Helsinki-starting route loop around the fixed [[Entities/Ruka]] / [[Entities/Solstice Festival]] anchor.
2. Compare reachable route families: Saimaa/Lakeland, archipelago/coast, West Coast, South Coast, and remote lake/island options.
3. Research kayaking, remote island stays, cabins, trail huts, and camping options that fit the rental-car window.
4. Understand lodging styles and which areas require early booking.
5. Capture current travel guidance, maps, ferry pages, lodging pages, festival details, and reference material as source notes.

## First Source-Backed Route Candidates

- [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]]: compact ferry-linked coastal route module; likely strongest first operational follow-up if the coast/archipelago theme is appealing.
- Saimaa / Savo-Karelia: major Lakeland route family with nature, food, castles, and towns.
- West Coast: coastal route family from Pori toward Oulu with beaches, wooden towns, and Kvarken/Hailuoto context.
- South Coast design and ironworks: shorter cultural route module near Helsinki/Turku.
- North Lapland: distinctive but currently likely unrealistic unless it becomes a top priority.
- Ruka / Kuusamo: fixed as the mid-trip northern anchor because of [[Entities/Solstice Festival]]; route planning should decide whether to approach through Saimaa/eastern Finland, the West Coast/Oulu corridor, or another staged route.

## Current Working Assumptions

- This is a roadtrip, so the vehicle and route logistics are central.
- The trip should stay spontaneous; the wiki should support informed choices rather than prescribe daily plans.
- Ferry, lodging, activity, road, and opening-hour facts should be sourced before they become commitments.
- The trip includes late June, so holiday periods and local closures should be checked before final bookings.
- Everyman's rights can support spontaneous low-impact nature access, but protected-area, campfire, fishing, dog, vehicle, and destination-specific rules still need checks before activity plans are treated as reliable.
- Finland's hut network may support hiking-oriented overnight options or rest stops, and huts/cabins are in scope. Open wilderness huts are shared and not guaranteed; paid huts need booking, key, and equipment checks.
- Camping gear is expected to be borrowed from a local friend, so camping is in scope but still depends on gear availability, weather, destination rules, and comfort.
- Kayaking on lakes and remote island stays are promising trip themes, but they need current provider, access, weather, safety, and lodging checks.
- Solstice operations add practical constraints: Ruka local movement is mostly foot/ski-lift/taxi/e-bike, the festival site is uneven fell terrain, there is no festival camping on site, and late-night return from the festival site requires walking after the lifts close.
