## Current User-Confirmed Route Frame

- Start and end: Helsinki.
- Arrival: plane to Helsinki.
- Car: rental car planned from 2026-06-06 through 2026-06-25, pickup at Helsinki Airport.
- Fixed anchor: [[Entities/Solstice Festival]] in [[Entities/Ruka]], Kuusamo, with a house booked from 2026-06-17 through either 2026-06-21 or 2026-06-22.
- Emerging interests: lake kayaking, packrafting, remote island stays, cabins, trail huts, and camping.
- Current interpretation: compare Helsinki-loop routes that reach Ruka without turning every day into a transfer day before reviving farther-north Lapland planning.

## Spontaneous Anchor Regions

- Turku archipelago / [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]]: ferry-linked route module with island detours and lodging/timetable dependencies.
- Saimaa / Savo-Karelia: dense cluster of lakes, ridges, castles, towns, food, and nature stops.
- South Coast design belt: ironworks villages, glass/design stops, and short-distance cultural alternatives.
- West Coast: wooden towns, beaches, maritime stops, Kvarken, and Oulu/Hailuoto.
- North Lapland: high-value but distance-heavy candidate if Arctic/Sámi/northern-road themes become central.
- Ruka / Kuusamo: fixed northern anchor; evaluate nearby nature, recovery, and route staging rather than treating it as optional.
- Lake and island adventure clusters: candidate theme for kayaking, packrafting, remote stays, huts, cabins, and flexible nature stops; needs region-specific provider, rule, and weather research.

## Comparison Criteria

- Driving load.
- Ferry dependency.
- Lodging flexibility.
- Nature access.
- Weather sensitivity.
- Cost.
- How much of the route stays interesting if plans change.
