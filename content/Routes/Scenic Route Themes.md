# Scenic Route Themes

This page collects route themes that could guide spontaneous decisions.

## Themes To Explore

- Coast and archipelago: [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]], West Coast wooden towns, beaches, and maritime landscapes.
- Lakes and forests: Saimaa/Lakeland, Punkaharju ridges, Road 62, Linnansaari, and Koli-related follow-up from guidebooks.
- Sheltered water activities: small lakes, rivers, near-shore paddling, and hike-plus-water modules that can be downgraded when wind or access rules are poor.
- National parks and hiking: Urho Kekkonen, Kevo, Linnansaari, Bothnian Sea, and later Luontoon/Nationalparks source checks.
- Ruka / Kuusamo northern anchor: festival base, fell setting, nearby lakes, outdoor access, and post-festival recovery route choices.
- Historic towns and cultural routes: Turku, Porvoo, South Coast ironworks villages, wooden towns, Häme Ox Road, and Lake Tuusula.
- Eastern borderland history: Lappeenranta, Imatra, Salpa Line, Mikkeli, and Karelia-related sites from the Lakeland route.
- Northern landscapes beyond Ruka, if the distance makes sense: Saariselkä, Inari, Karigasniemi, Utsjoki, Nuorgam, Teno river road.
- Design, architecture, and city stops: Fiskars, Iittala, Riihimäki, Mathildedal, Lake Tuusula, Helsinki-area add-ons.
