# Scenic Route Themes

This page collects route themes that could guide spontaneous decisions.

## Themes To Explore

- Coast and archipelago: [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]], [[Entities/Turku]], West Coast wooden towns, beaches, Hailuoto, and maritime landscapes.
- Lakes and forests: [[Entities/Saimaa]], [[Entities/Savonlinna]], Punkaharju ridges, Road 62, [[Entities/Linnansaari National Park]], [[Entities/Kolovesi National Park]], and Koli-related follow-up from guidebooks.
- Eastern route spine: [[Entities/Saimaa]], [[Entities/Koli National Park]], [[Entities/Kuhmo]], [[Entities/Kainuu]], [[Entities/Hossa National Park]], and [[Entities/Ruka]] as candidate pre-festival route branches.
- Sheltered water activities: small lakes, rivers, near-shore paddling, and hike-plus-water modules that can be downgraded when wind or access rules are poor.
- National parks and hiking: [[Entities/Hossa National Park]], [[Entities/Oulanka National Park]], [[Entities/Koli National Park]], [[Entities/Linnansaari National Park]], [[Entities/Kolovesi National Park]], [[Entities/Archipelago National Park]], [[Entities/Bothnian Sea National Park]], and [[Entities/Urho Kekkonen National Park]].
- Ruka / Kuusamo northern anchor: festival base, fell setting, nearby lakes, outdoor access, and post-festival recovery route choices.
- Historic towns and cultural routes: [[Entities/Turku]], Porvoo, South Coast ironworks villages, wooden towns, Hame Ox Road, and Lake Tuusula.
- Eastern borderland history: Lappeenranta, Imatra, Salpa Line, Mikkeli, and Karelia-related sites from the Lakeland route.
- Northern landscapes beyond Ruka, if the distance makes sense: [[Entities/Lapland]], Saariselka, Inari, Karigasniemi, Utsjoki, Nuorgam, Teno river road.
- Design, architecture, and city stops: Fiskars, Iittala, Riihimäki, Mathildedal, Lake Tuusula, Helsinki-area add-ons.
