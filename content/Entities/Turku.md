# Turku

Turku is the main southwest city and archipelago gateway in the current route research. The Lonely Planet 11th edition places it in both the Essential Suomi and Cities & Coasts route logic, while [[Sources/The Archipelago Trail]] makes the surrounding archipelago a separate ferry-linked module.

## Planning Role

- Candidate southern/coastal add-on near the beginning or end of the trip.
- Gateway if [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]] or a smaller archipelago detour survives route pruning.
- More useful as a flexible module than as a core route spine to [[Entities/Ruka]].
