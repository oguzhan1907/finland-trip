# Archipelago National Park

Archipelago National Park is the protected-area layer behind some Turku archipelago and outer-island ideas. Tourism sources may also describe this area as the Archipelago Sea National Park. In this vault it matters because the [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]] and Small Archipelago Trail can create tempting island, ferry, kayaking, and camping ideas that are not the same thing as ordinary roadside travel.

For this trip, the park is a **coastal nature and ferry-linked add-on candidate**, not the main route spine. It becomes relevant if the trip chooses a small coastal opener, outer-island detour, or archipelago camping/kayaking idea.

## Planning Role

- Candidate protected-area context for the full or Small [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]].
- Possible fit for outer-island nature, ferry-linked day trips, camping, and guided sea kayaking.
- Most realistic as an early-trip coastal module if archipelago remains in scope.
- Needs ferry, weather, island access, camping, fire, and provider checks before use.

## What It Could Add

- A real protected-area nature layer to the archipelago route, beyond villages and ferry crossings.
- Outer-island character if Noto, Aspo, Jurmo, Uto, or similar islands become serious candidates.
- A clear reason to separate "Archipelago Trail by car" from "national-park island access."

## Operational Checks

- Official Luontoon instructions and rules.
- Island-specific camping, fire, waste, water, and restricted-area rules.
- Current commuter ferry routes and whether the desired island is reachable with the available time.
- Sea-kayaking exposure, weather limits, and guide/provider availability.
- Whether this complements the Small Archipelago Trail or turns it into a more complex outer-archipelago plan.
