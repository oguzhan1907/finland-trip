# Archipelago Trail

The Archipelago Trail is a ferry-linked coastal route in the Turku archipelago. In this vault it matters as a possible 3-5 day route module for the June 2026 Finland roadtrip and as the first concrete route family where ferries are part of the travel experience rather than just transit.

[[Sources/Lonely Planet Finland 11th Edition]] keeps the [[Entities/Turku]] / archipelago direction alive as a southern/coastal route module, but the Ruka anchor still makes it an add-on or comparison path rather than the current default.

[[Entities/Archipelago National Park]] is the protected-area layer to check if the archipelago idea becomes more than a car/ferry route and starts involving outer islands, camping, kayaking, or national-park access.

## Why It Matters

- The source page states that the full trail season in 2026 overlaps the trip window.
- The route creates a strong coast-and-archipelago theme without requiring the trip to go far north.
- It depends on ferry timing, car access, and local lodging, so it needs operational follow-up before being treated as a commitment.

## Planning Notes

- Full trail: 250 km according to the source.
- Small Archipelago Trail: 120 km according to the source.
- Recommended minimum duration: 3-5 days according to the source.
- Named regions include Pargas, Nagu, Korpo, Houtskär, Iniö, Kustavi, Taivassalo, Vehmaa, Mynämäki, and Naantali.
