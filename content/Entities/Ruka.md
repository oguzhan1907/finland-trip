# Ruka

Ruka is the Kuusamo resort area that hosts [[Entities/Solstice Festival]] at Rukatunturi / Ruka Ski Resort. It now matters as the roadtrip's fixed mid-trip geographic anchor. The route has to reach Ruka before the festival window and leave enough time after the festival to return the rental car in Helsinki on 2026-06-25.

The Lonely Planet 11th edition adds surrounding-route context: Ruka is close to [[Entities/Oulanka National Park]], within reach of Kuusamo-area bear/wildlife activity leads, and can connect toward [[Entities/Hossa National Park]]. Those leads need current checks and should not override the festival logistics.

## Planning Role

- Mid-trip anchor around 2026-06-17 through either 2026-06-21 or 2026-06-22.
- Northern / northeastern route target for the Helsinki rental-car loop.
- Base for festival lodging, parking, groceries, pharmacy access, ski-lift or foot movement, and post-festival recovery planning.

## Operational Notes

- The Solstice source places the event at Rukatunturi / Ruka Ski Resort in Kuusamo.
- The Resident Advisor ticketing page lists the venue address as Rukatunturintie 9, 93825 Ruka, Finland.
- Local movement around the festival is mainly by foot and ski lift; late-night return from the festival site requires walking down after the lift closes.
- There is no festival-organized camping on site; tent camping near Ruka needs separate destination-rule verification.
- Nearby Oulanka/Hossa/Kuusamo activity leads should be treated as optional recovery or route modules until house checkout, fatigue, and current provider/rule details are known.
