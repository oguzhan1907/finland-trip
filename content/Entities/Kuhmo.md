# Kuhmo

Kuhmo is a southeastern [[Entities/Kainuu]] route candidate that Lonely Planet frames around taiga wildlife, wilderness huts, Kalevala cultural material, and Winter War history. It matters because it gives Option A a stronger identity after [[Entities/Koli National Park]] than "drive north through Kainuu."

## Planning Role

- Candidate bridge between [[Entities/Koli National Park]] and the broader Kainuu/Ruka approach.
- Potential wildlife/culture stop if the route can absorb the extra bend.
- Needs current provider, lodging, ethical wildlife, and drive-time checks before becoming operational.
