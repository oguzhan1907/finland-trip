# Solstice Festival

Solstice Festival is now a fixed trip anchor for the Finland roadtrip. The user provided the festival website, <https://solsticefestival.fi/>, and confirmed that a house is booked for the festival window from 2026-06-17 through either 2026-06-21 or 2026-06-22.

The official festival pages now place Solstice 2026 at Rukatunturi / Ruka Ski Resort in [[Entities/Ruka]], Kuusamo, with the public festival dates listed as 2026-06-18 through 2026-06-20. The linked ticketing page lists the venue address as Rukatunturintie 9, 93825 Ruka, Finland.

## Planning Role

- Fixed mid-trip anchor between the Helsinki start and Helsinki end.
- Route planning should work backward from arrival in [[Entities/Ruka]] before 2026-06-17 and forward from departure on either 2026-06-21 or 2026-06-22.
- The exact house checkout date changes how much time remains before returning the rental car on 2026-06-25.
- Any nearby route, lodging, food, parking, or recovery-day plan should be checked against festival logistics.
- The festival site is a rocky fell-top environment with level changes; local movement is mainly by foot and ski lift.
- There is no camping on the festival site or organized by the festival.

## Operational Notes

- Festival hours are listed as 16:00-02:00, with After Hours 01:00-06:00 at ski lodge Piste in Ruka Village.
- Three-day festival tickets include a ski-lift ticket; one-day tickets do not.
- Ski lifts close at 20:00, so late-night return from the festival site requires walking down a marked road.
- Driving to Ruka is possible and official guidance says there are parking spaces around Ruka Village and some accommodations.
- Outside drinks/food are not allowed on the festival site except small snacks and an empty non-deposit water bottle.
- Fresh drinking water is available inside the festival area.
- Packing needs include warm layers, wind- and waterproof clothing, suitable shoes, mosquito protection, sunscreen, and sun protection.
