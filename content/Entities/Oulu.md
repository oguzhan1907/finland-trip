# Oulu

Oulu is the main city anchor for the west-coast / Northern Ostrobothnia comparison route. Lonely Planet highlights it as a northern city, 2026 culture-year destination, Hailuoto gateway, and possible stepping stone toward [[Entities/Oulanka National Park]].

[[Entities/Bothnian Sea National Park]] is the current national-park page for the broader West Coast comparison family. It matters only if the route shifts toward Pori/Yyteri, maritime islands, or an Oulu-forward coastal approach.

## Planning Role

- Candidate western corridor stop if the route goes coast -> Oulu -> Oulanka/Ruka.
- Possible post-festival or pre-festival comparison path, but not currently the best match for the lake/cabin/hut focus.
- Needs current event, lodging, ferry, and route geometry checks if the west corridor returns to the shortlist.
- Related coastal nature page: [[Entities/Bothnian Sea National Park]].
