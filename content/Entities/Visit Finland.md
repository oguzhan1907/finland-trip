# Visit Finland

Visit Finland is Finland's national tourism promotion source. In this vault it matters as a route-inspiration and destination-discovery source, especially for broad trip themes and regional tourism links.

## Why It Matters

- Provides source material for early route family comparison.
- Links onward to regional tourism pages that may be useful for deeper route-specific ingestion.
- Should be treated as inspirational and tourism-oriented rather than as the final authority for current schedules, road rules, ferry bookings, or opening hours.
