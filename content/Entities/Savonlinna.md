# Savonlinna

Savonlinna is a Saimaa/Lakeland stop candidate that Lonely Planet repeatedly uses as part of the lake, castle, boat-trip, and Punkaharju route cluster. It matters because it could become one of the concrete anchors inside the otherwise broad [[Entities/Saimaa]] trunk.

## Planning Role

- Candidate Saimaa overnight or activity base.
- Possible link to Punkaharju, [[Entities/Linnansaari National Park]], [[Entities/Kolovesi National Park]], and lake/boat/paddling ideas.
- Needs current lodging, parking, boat, and activity checks before becoming operational.
