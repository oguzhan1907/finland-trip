# Koli National Park

Koli National Park is a North Karelia route candidate in the source pack's eastern spine. It matters because the Visit Finland eastern route uses Koli as a major scenic/cultural anchor between Joensuu and Kajaani, which could connect an eastern route family toward Kainuu and [[Entities/Ruka]].

The Lonely Planet 11th edition strengthens Koli's value case by presenting it as a major national-landscape and hiking stop and by linking it onward to [[Entities/Kuhmo]]. This makes the Koli branch more attractive but also raises the need to prove the extra bend works before the Ruka deadline.

## Planning Role

- Candidate scenic/nature anchor if the route goes through Joensuu / North Karelia / Kajaani.
- More likely a route-shaping stop than a paddling/lodging anchor at this stage.
- Needs full Luontoon rules/directions and lodging/parking checks before it becomes operational.
- Strongest role is Option A: [[Entities/Saimaa]] -> Koli -> [[Entities/Kuhmo]] / [[Entities/Kainuu]] -> [[Entities/Ruka]].

## What It Could Add

- A strong eastern scenic identity that is different from Saimaa lake scenery and Ruka/Kuusamo festival terrain.
- A reason for the route to bend through North Karelia rather than using a simpler Kuopio / Kajaani staging spine.
- A cultural-landscape argument for Option A, especially if the trip wants iconic scenery and hiking more than water activity.

## Operational Checks

- Official Luontoon instructions, rules, trail, and parking guidance.
- Whether a short visit gives enough value or whether Koli needs an overnight nearby.
- Lodging and parking pressure if the visit falls near midsummer travel.
- Whether Koli combines cleanly with [[Entities/Kuhmo]] or makes the pre-Ruka route too long.
- Weather/fog fallback, since Koli's value is partly viewpoint-driven.
