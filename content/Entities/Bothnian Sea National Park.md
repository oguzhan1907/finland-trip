# Bothnian Sea National Park

Bothnian Sea National Park is a west-coast protected-area candidate tied to the Pori-to-Oulu / maritime route family. It matters because the current route sources keep the West Coast and [[Entities/Oulu]] corridor alive as a comparison path, even though the eastern / Saimaa / Ruka route is currently stronger.

For this trip, Bothnian Sea is a **comparison-route park**. It should not receive deep operational research unless the route shifts toward the West Coast, Pori/Yyteri, wooden towns, islands, or an Oulu-forward approach to [[Entities/Ruka]].

## Planning Role

- Candidate natural-area context for the West Coast route family.
- Possible fit for maritime landscapes, islands, boating, and coastal stops.
- Secondary priority because the current trip interests lean more toward Saimaa, cabins, huts, and Ruka-adjacent outdoor planning.
- Needs current access, water transport, service, and island-rule checks if the West Coast route returns to the shortlist.

## What It Could Add

- A protected-area anchor for the west-coast comparison route.
- A nature counterweight to coastal towns, beaches, and Oulu/Hailuoto staging.
- A reason to test whether west-coast maritime travel can compete with Saimaa lake travel.

## Operational Checks

- Official Luontoon instructions and rules.
- Whether meaningful park access is practical by car alone or needs boat transport.
- Island access, camping, fire, waste, and service details.
- Weather exposure and whether activities require local providers.
- Fit against the Ruka deadline and the long west-to-east turn toward Kuusamo.
