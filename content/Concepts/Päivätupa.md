# Päivätupa

**Päivätupa** means a day-use hut. In this vault, treat it as daytime rest infrastructure, not planned lodging. It can be useful for lunch, warmth, drying gear, weather breaks, and route contingency, but it should not become an overnight plan except where an official source explicitly allows emergency use.

## Planning Meaning

- Good day-hike support and bad-weather contingency.
- Not a normal lodging substitute.
- May be open and unlocked, but the current destination page still controls usage, equipment, fire/stove rules, and emergency overnight assumptions.
- Useful when comparing hikes that need rest stops without committing to a hut night.

## Relationship To Other Terms

- Less overnight-relevant than [[Concepts/Autiotupa]].
- Less booking-relevant than [[Concepts/Varaustupa]].
- Can overlap functionally with [[Concepts/Laavu]] or [[Concepts/Kota]] as a rest stop, but the structure and rules may differ.

## Route Use

Use päivätupa as a map-reading term for day hikes around Hossa, Oulanka, Koli, Saimaa, and Lapland. It can improve a hiking route's comfort without increasing lodging commitment.
