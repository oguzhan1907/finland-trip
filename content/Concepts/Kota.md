# Kota

**Kota** can mean a hut or lavvu-style shelter, often associated with a fire pit, stove, or central hearth. In this vault, treat kota as a shelter/fire-use lead rather than a lodging guarantee. The exact meaning can vary between a fixed public shelter, a reservable shelter, or a tent-style structure.

## Planning Meaning

- Useful as a shelter, cooking, fire, or weather-contingency lead.
- Fire and camping rules still apply.
- In protected areas, the destination's instructions and rules control where camping and stove/fire use are allowed.
- Wildfire warnings and local firewood rules can invalidate assumptions even when a kota exists.

## Relationship To Other Terms

- Different from [[Concepts/Autiotupa]], which is specifically an open wilderness hut.
- Different from [[Concepts/Varaustupa]], which is a reservable hut concept.
- May be used more like a rest/fire shelter alongside [[Concepts/Laavu]] or [[Concepts/Päivätupa]], depending on the site.

## Route Use

Use kota as a sign to check shelter and fire rules, not as a green light to camp or cook. It is especially relevant for route regions where temporary camping, official campfire sites, or shelter-based breaks are being considered.
