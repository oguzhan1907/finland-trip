# Finnish Hut And Shelter Terms

This page is a working glossary for Finnish outdoor accommodation terms that may appear on Luontoon, Nationalparks, Eraluvat, provider pages, maps, and signs. It should help route planning avoid confusing free shared huts, paid reservable huts, day shelters, and fire shelters. The operational rule is simple: translate the term, then check the specific destination page before assuming overnight permission, booking status, fire rules, equipment, bedding, access, or capacity.

## Core Terms

| Finnish term | Working English meaning | Concept page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **autiotupa**; plural **autiotuvat** | Open wilderness hut | [[Concepts/Autiotupa]] |
| **varaustupa** | Reservable hut | [[Concepts/Varaustupa]] |
| **päivätupa** | Day-use hut | [[Concepts/Päivätupa]] |
| **laavu** | Lean-to shelter / open shelter | [[Concepts/Laavu]] |
| **kota** | Hut or lavvu-style shelter, often with a fire pit or stove | [[Concepts/Kota]] |

## Mapping To Existing Source Categories

[[Sources/Huts - Hiking Tips]] already supports the English category split:

- open wilderness huts
- shared rental huts
- rental huts
- day-use huts
- rental campfire huts

The Finnish terms help when reading maps and provider pages, but they are not enough by themselves. A hut labeled with a familiar term can still have local restrictions, seasonal access, missing equipment, booking rules, or fire restrictions.

## Route Planning Use

- Use [[Concepts/Autiotupa]] / autiotuvat as a flexibility signal, not a booked accommodation plan.
- Use [[Concepts/Varaustupa]] as a booking lead if a hike or remote stop needs reliability.
- Use [[Concepts/Päivätupa]], [[Concepts/Laavu]], and [[Concepts/Kota]] as rest, cooking, or weather-contingency leads until the specific site rules say otherwise.
- Record any hut that becomes route-relevant in [[Lodging/Booking Watchlist]] before treating it as part of the plan.
- For Hossa, Oulanka, Koli, Saimaa, or Lapland routes, verify the exact hut type against current Luontoon / Nationalparks / Eraluvat pages.
