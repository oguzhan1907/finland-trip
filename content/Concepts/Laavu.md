# Laavu

**Laavu** means a lean-to shelter or open shelter. In this vault, treat it as a rest, cooking, or simple weather-shelter lead, not as automatic overnight permission. A laavu may be useful on hikes or at campfire sites, but its use depends on the exact destination rules.

## Planning Meaning

- Useful as a route comfort and contingency feature.
- May support rest, meals, fire-site use, or basic shelter depending on the site.
- Does not automatically mean sleeping is allowed.
- Requires current checks for fire permission, wildfire warnings, firewood rules, camping/overnight permission, and protected-area restrictions.

## Relationship To Other Terms

- Less lodging-like than [[Concepts/Autiotupa]].
- Less bookable than [[Concepts/Varaustupa]].
- Can function like a simpler rest shelter compared with [[Concepts/Päivätupa]].
- Related to [[Concepts/Kota]] as a fire/shelter term, but the structure and rules differ by site.

## Route Use

Use laavu as a map-reading term for hikes, picnic stops, rain breaks, and possible fire-site checks. If a route depends on a laavu, verify the official destination page before assuming fire, camping, or overnight use.
