# Autiotupa

**Autiotupa** means an open wilderness hut; the plural is **autiotuvat**. In this vault, treat an autiotupa as free, unlocked, shared hut infrastructure for self-guided hikers, usually intended for short overnight stays. It can make a route more flexible, but it is not a guaranteed bed and should not be treated like a booking.

## Planning Meaning

- Good flexibility signal for hiking or remote nature stops.
- Usually first-come-first-served and shared with other hikers.
- Better as a fallback or opportunistic stop than as a fixed accommodation plan.
- Needs a destination-specific check for access, equipment, capacity, firewood, dry toilet, nearby water, seasonal access, and any local restrictions.

## Relationship To Other Terms

- More flexible but less reliable than [[Concepts/Varaustupa]].
- Different from [[Concepts/Päivätupa]], which is primarily daytime infrastructure.
- May appear near [[Concepts/Laavu]] or [[Concepts/Kota]] shelters on maps, but those terms do not imply the same overnight rules.

## Route Use

Use autiotupa/autiotuvat as a search and map-reading term when checking [[Entities/Hossa National Park]], [[Entities/Oulanka National Park]], [[Entities/Koli National Park]], [[Entities/Saimaa]], or [[Entities/Lapland]]. If a specific hut becomes route-relevant, add it to [[Lodging/Booking Watchlist]] with the current official page.
