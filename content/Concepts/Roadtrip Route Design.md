# Roadtrip Route Design

Route design is a research problem for this vault: the trip needs to fit the fixed window from 2026-06-04 to 2026-06-26 while preserving spontaneity. The route should be understood as a set of possible route families, anchor regions, ferry constraints, lodging risks, and optional stop clusters rather than a fixed daily itinerary. For this trip, the most important current decisions are how to shape the Helsinki-to-[[Entities/Ruka]] loop, how ambitious the pre-festival approach should be, and which route choices would reduce flexibility.

The first route-source batch points toward a **route-family comparison** rather than a single best route. [[Sources/Discover 5 Extraordinary Road Trips in Finland]] provides five internal Finland route families with very different tradeoffs: North Lapland, Savo-Karelia/Lakeland, Lake Tuusula, South Coast design/ironworks, and West Coast Pori-Oulu. [[Sources/The Archipelago Trail]] adds a sixth candidate: a ferry-linked Turku archipelago route that appears compact enough to become a strong southern/coastal anchor, but only after ferry and lodging checks.

The 2026-05-16 planning update narrows the shape: the roadtrip starts and ends in Helsinki, the car is planned for 2026-06-06 through 2026-06-25, and [[Entities/Solstice Festival]] is a fixed mid-trip anchor from 2026-06-17 through either 2026-06-21 or 2026-06-22. That makes the next comparison a Helsinki-loop problem, not an international entry-route problem. [[Queries/Helsinki Ruka Route Comparison]] currently points to an eastern / Saimaa / Kainuu approach before the festival, with a simpler central or direct return after the festival.

## Current Planning Logic

- Fix route anchors before optimizing individual stops.
- Treat Helsinki, the rental-car window, [[Entities/Ruka]], and [[Entities/Solstice Festival]] as the current hard frame.
- Keep route ideas tentative until the main entry and return route families are known.
- Avoid a route that depends on perfect weather, perfect ferry timing, or every day running exactly as planned.
- Treat article route durations as module sizes, not final itinerary promises.
- Separate route inspiration from operational sources: ferry timetables, opening hours, lodging availability, and current road rules still need source checks.

## Current Route-Family Tension

- [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]] looks efficient and distinctive, but it introduces ferry timing and lodging dependencies and may not fit the Ruka route geometry.
- Savo-Karelia / Saimaa offers the strongest major inland nature-and-culture route and currently looks like the best pre-festival research direction, but the source's 7-day shape may still need pruning.
- North Lapland offers the strongest northern identity theme, but anything beyond Ruka/Kuusamo could make the roadtrip less flexible.
- South Coast, Lake Tuusula, and West Coast modules may work well as add-ons or alternate route families depending on the final Helsinki loop.
- Lake kayaking and remote island stays are now a user-interest route theme, but need source work before they can compete with the existing route families.
