# Varaustupa

**Varaustupa** means a reservable hut. In this vault, treat it as a paid or reservation-based hut lead that can make a hiking or remote stop more reliable than [[Concepts/Autiotupa]], but only after current booking, key, equipment, and cancellation details are checked.

## Planning Meaning

- Useful when a route needs a reliable hut night rather than first-come-first-served uncertainty.
- Usually implies booking/payment, but the exact booking model can vary by hut and provider.
- Requires current checks for availability, key pickup, bedding, cookware, stove, firewood, dry toilet, water, access distance, and cancellation terms.
- Should be added to [[Lodging/Booking Watchlist]] before it becomes a route commitment.

## Relationship To Other Terms

- More reliable than [[Concepts/Autiotupa]], but less spontaneous.
- Different from [[Concepts/Päivätupa]], which is normally a daytime rest hut.
- If a map also shows [[Concepts/Laavu]] or [[Concepts/Kota]], do not assume those shelters are equivalent substitutes for a reserved hut.

## Route Use

Use varaustupa as a booking term when evaluating hut-supported hiking near Hossa, Oulanka, Koli, Saimaa, or Lapland. A varaustupa can reduce lodging risk, but it can also make the route less spontaneous if it fixes a night too early.
