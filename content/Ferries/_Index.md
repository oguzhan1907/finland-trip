## Research Goals

- Identify vehicle-compatible domestic, coastal, archipelago, lake, and island ferry options.
- Compare cost, schedule, comfort, flexibility, and booking risk.
- Track ports and operators without committing to exact dates yet.
- Keep international ferry entry/exit research low priority unless the route frame changes; focus on Finnish archipelago, lake, and island transfers that affect the Helsinki-to-[[Entities/Ruka]] loop.
