# Ferry Comparison Criteria

Use these criteria when comparing ferry options.

## Criteria

- Total route time, including drive to port and onward drive.
- Vehicle rules and dimensions.
- Schedule fit with a spontaneous roadtrip.
- Cabin or overnight comfort.
- Cancellation and amendment rules.
- Price and price volatility.
- Port convenience.
- Weather sensitivity.
- Whether the crossing itself adds to the trip.
- Whether a ferry dependency is the point of the route, as with [[Entities/Archipelago Trail]], or only a way to move the car between countries.
- Whether there is a practical fallback if a seasonal archipelago crossing is full, delayed, or not running.
